# A calibration records desk without a front end

The quality coordinator asks for the recall list, records certificate evidence, reviews out-of-tolerance cases and prints the resulting worksheets. A database and a coding agent can do that desk work without a screen to maintain.

A mobile scanner, offline kiosk, barcode label workflow, validated electronic signature, laboratory instrument connection, uncertainty calculator and measurement-system study each serves a different purpose. This base does not include them. It does not collect measurements or issue calibration certificates. It renders read-only recall sheets, evidence summaries and impact worksheets. Those reports remain snapshots until regenerated.

For an ordinary manufacturer coordinating external calibration and keeping tool history, the records desk is useful on its own. If the business depends on regulated electronic approvals or automated metrology, retain that system until the replacement is validated for the intended use. Enterprise DNA can scope screens, connections and approval controls around the records with the customer and their quality specialists.

The same database works with Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode or Cursor. One trusted operator uses the base. Multi-user identity, authorisation, backups and operational support belong in the managed installation.
